Workflow automation

We automate the manual handoffs hiding between your tools: approvals, updates, reporting, reminders, data syncs, and task creation.

What we do

Most companies don't have a software problem. They have a workflow problem. The real cost isn't the SaaS bills; it's the human hours spent moving data between systems that should talk to each other.

Workflows we typically automate

  • Sales operations. Lead routing, CRM enrichment, deal-stage progression, contract generation, post-sale handoffs.
  • Customer onboarding. Account provisioning, welcome sequences, document collection, training scheduling.
  • Finance and ops. Invoice processing, expense approvals, reconciliation, monthly reporting.
  • HR and people. Hiring pipelines, offer letters, onboarding sequences, offboarding checklists.

What you get

  • Full workflow audit and mapping
  • Prioritised automation roadmap
  • Built and tested automations in 2 to 6 weeks
  • Monitoring and alerting
  • Team training so your ops people can maintain them

Typical impact

For ops-heavy companies, the first automation sprint typically eliminates a significant portion of weekly manual handoffs within 60 days.
